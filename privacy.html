put.io Audiobooks

Privacy

This applies to the web app at putio-audiobooks.vercel.app and the Android app, which opens the same web app.

No server in between

The app talks to put.io straight from your device. There is no server of ours that sees your files, your token or what you listen to.

What is stored, and where

  • Connecting gives the app a token for your whole put.io account, not just one folder. The app only lists files, reads audio and stores its own settings.
  • Your token stays on your device.
  • Listening positions, stars and hidden books are kept on your device and synced through your put.io account’s per-app storage.
  • Downloaded books are kept in the app’s storage on that device, until you remove them from a book’s menu or clear the app’s data.

What is sent elsewhere

  • Cover art: the title of each book without a cover is sent to Apple and Open Library once, after a scan, and so is whatever you search for in the cover picker.
  • Page views are counted with Vercel Web Analytics: no cookies, and nothing about your library or put.io account.

Nothing is sold or shared for advertising.
